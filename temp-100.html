Rating schedule notes

The notes that are easiest to overlook, quoted from public sources.

Prosthetic implants and resurfacing, DCs 5051-5056: current notes

Law 38 C.F.R. § 4.71a, eCFR text as of Feb 27, 2026. These notes took effect Feb 7, 2021 (85 FR 76453).

  1. Note (1): When an evaluation is assigned for joint resurfacing or the prosthetic replacement of a joint under diagnostic codes 5051-5056, an additional rating under § 4.71a may not also be assigned for that joint, unless otherwise directed.
  2. Note (2): Only evaluate a revision procedure in the same manner as the original procedure under diagnostic codes 5051-5056 if all the original components are replaced.
  3. Note (3): The term “prosthetic replacement” in diagnostic codes 5051-5053 and 5055-5056 means a total replacement of the named joint. However, in DC 5054, “prosthetic replacement” means a total replacement of the head of the femur or of the acetabulum.
  4. Note (4): The 100 percent rating for 1 year following implantation of prosthesis will commence after initial grant of the 1-month total rating assigned under § 4.30 following hospital discharge.
  5. Note (5): The 100 percent rating for 4 months following implantation of prosthesis or resurfacing under DCs 5054 and 5055 will commence after initial grant of the 1-month total rating assigned under § 4.30 following hospital discharge.
  6. Note (6): Special monthly compensation is assignable during the 100 percent rating period the earliest date permanent use of crutches is established.

DC 5054, hip resurfacing or replacement

Law 38 C.F.R. § 4.71a, DC 5054

  • Note: At the conclusion of the 100 percent evaluation period, evaluate resurfacing under diagnostic codes 5250 through 5255; there is no minimum evaluation for resurfacing.

Prosthetic implants: notes before Feb 7, 2021

Law 38 C.F.R. § 4.71a, eCFR text as of Feb 4, 2021. Surgeries before Feb 7, 2021 are governed by this version.

  1. Note (1): The 100 pct rating for 1 year following implantation of prosthesis will commence after initial grant of the 1-month total rating assigned under § 4.30 following hospital discharge.
  2. Note (2): Special monthly compensation is assignable during the 100 pct rating period the earliest date permanent use of crutches is established.

Hospital stays over 21 days, § 4.29

VA guidance M21-1 VIII.iv.8.B.1.a, public VA manual, not binding law

  • Provided a total, 100-percent rating cannot be assigned under other provisions of the rating schedule, assign a temporary 100-percent evaluation under 38 CFR 4.29 for periods of hospitalization at a Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) or an approved hospital or hospital observation at VA expense in excess of 21 days for medical treatment of a service-connected (SC) disability, or a disability requiring hospitalization in excess of 21 days for medical treatment for which compensation is payable under either 38 U.S.C. 1151 or 38 U.S.C. 1160.
  • Note: Competency must be addressed when assigning a temporary 100-percent evaluation for a mental disorder under 38 CFR 4.29.
  • Note: Hospitalization in a uniformed services facility (also known as military treatment facility) is considered an approved hospitalization for the purpose of determining entitlement to a temporary evaluation under 38 CFR 4.29.
