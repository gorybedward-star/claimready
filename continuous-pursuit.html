VA Continuous Pursuit Review

Describe your claim history in your own words. The tool rebuilds the timeline, checks each review deadline, and explains whether continuous pursuit appears to have been kept.

Your claim history

For each filing, say what kind it was: supplemental claim, Higher-Level Review, Board appeal, or court appeal. Approximate or missing dates are fine, but they may need to be verified, and they can change the result.

Do not include personal identifiers. Leave out your Social Security number, VA file or claim number, date of birth, and address. They are not needed. What you type stays in this browser. This version reads your history with on-device rules and does not send it to any AI service or server, and nothing is saved after you close the page.
